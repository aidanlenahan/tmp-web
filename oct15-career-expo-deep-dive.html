Every employer at today's session, researched directly from their own sites and internship postings — what they do, actual internship programs where they exist, and whether a freshman can apply now or needs to wait.

Quick read on freshman eligibility: most structured corporate internship programs (EXL, Con Edison, Jacobs) want you to have finished sophomore year at minimum, and MIT Lincoln Lab and Wall Street summer-analyst programs (Natixis) effectively want juniors. Mirae Asset Securities explicitly lists freshmen as eligible — that's your best confirmed shot at an internship application this cycle. For everyone else marked "ask directly," the smaller or less formal the company, the more likely they'll take a motivated freshman who shows up in person — worth asking regardless of a posted internship.
